# M4 report: an NMS-free model (YOLOv10n), accuracy and throughput

**What M4 is:** a descriptive appendix with no hypotheses. It sets YOLOv10n, a model that needs no NMS, next to M1's and M2's YOLOv8n on the same board:
- their accuracy on COCO val2017, INT8 on the board's NPU and FP32 on the PC;
- the NPU's capacity for each;
- each model in C's pipeline at M2's two points.

What is measured, how, and what is reported were registered before any measurement ([prereg/M4.md](../../prereg/M4.md)). **Nothing here is a verdict.** M4 has no decision rule, and it does not say which model is better (§7).

| | YOLOv8n | YOLOv10n |
|---|---|---|
| AP@[.5:.95], INT8 on the board | 36.8 | 37.8 |
| AP@[.5:.95], FP32 on the PC | 37.3 | 38.4 |
| INT8 − FP32 | −0.51 points | −0.63 points |
| `P_cap`, 3 NPU cores, with its pipeline post-processing | 193.2 inferences/s | 161.8 inferences/s |
| `rknn_run` p50, 8 channels × 5 fps | 15.0 ms | 17.9 ms |
| Latency p50, 8 channels × 5 fps | 16.9 ms | 19.8 ms |
| Process CPU, 8 channels × 5 fps | 17.80 s | 18.07 s |
| Process CPU, 5 channels × 30 fps | 33.25 s | 35.04 s (saturated) |

AP is over all 5,000 images, with §2.3's rule. CPU is the mean of 3 runs per 120 s window. At 5 channels × 30 fps, YOLOv10n's runs are labelled saturated (below), so that comparison is between unequal loads.

## What was compared

| | YOLOv8n (arm C8) | YOLOv10n (arm C10) |
|---|---|---|
| INT8 model (board) | `yolov8n_i8.rknn`, `04a76611…`, M1's and M2's | `yolov10n_i8.rknn`, `6a6442e1…` |
| FP32 reference (PC) | `yolov8n.onnx`, `0c871670…` | `yolov10n.onnx`, `4a925bcb…` |
| Post-processing for mAP (§2.3) | multi-label, class-wise greedy NMS at IoU 0.7 | `yolov10_post`, the 300 best (cell, class) pairs, no NMS |
| Pipeline post-processing (§3.2) | Cp's `NeonPost` (M2), conf 0.25, NMS 0.45 | `yolov10_post`, conf 0.25, at most 300, no NMS |

- **Origin:** both models come from rknn_model_zoo `v2.3.2`. They were converted in the same pinned image with the same 20 quantization images. The files are in the Releases [`data-model-yolov8n_i8-04a76611`](https://github.com/eunkyo3/rk3588s-zero-copy/releases/tag/data-model-yolov8n_i8-04a76611) and [`data-model-yolov10n_i8-6a6442e1`](https://github.com/eunkyo3/rk3588s-zero-copy/releases/tag/data-model-yolov10n_i8-6a6442e1).
- **The mAP rule:** conf 0.001 and at most 300 detections per image, Ultralytics' val defaults. Each rule is implemented twice:
  - in C++ on the board, for INT8;
  - in Python on the PC, for FP32.

  Before `freeze-m4`, the two gave the same detections on the first 50 images (A14).
- **The pipeline arms:** both are variant C from the same binary. Only the model and its post-processing differ. C8 is M2's arm Cp.

## Accuracy

COCOeval, `iouType` bbox, default parameters, over the 5,000 images. Values are in percent.

| Model | Precision | AP@[.5:.95] | AP50 | AP75 | AP small | AP medium | AP large | AR@100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YOLOv8n | INT8 | 36.8 | 51.9 | 40.1 | 18.3 | 40.6 | 52.7 | 56.9 |
| | FP32 | 37.3 | 52.6 | 40.5 | 18.7 | 41.0 | 53.3 | 59.3 |
| YOLOv10n | INT8 | 37.8 | 52.7 | 41.0 | 18.6 | 41.6 | 54.7 | 57.6 |
| | FP32 | 38.4 | 53.5 | 41.7 | 19.0 | 42.1 | 54.8 | 60.3 |

- **Quantization cost, INT8 − FP32:**
  - YOLOv8n: −0.51 points of AP@[.5:.95], −0.68 of AP50.
  - YOLOv10n: −0.63 and −0.77.
  - AR@100 falls more: −2.37 and −2.66 points.
- **Without the 20 quantization images** (4,980 images): every AP@[.5:.95] and AP50 is within 0.03 points of its 5,000-image value. The 5,000-image values are the main ones.
- **The published values,** obtained under other settings: YOLOv8n 37.3 and YOLOv10n 38.5. M4's FP32 values, rounded to 0.1, are 37.3 and 38.4, so §5's trigger, a difference above 2.0 points, did not fire.
- **Detections kept:**
  - Both INT8 passes kept 300 detections on every image. On 4,984 (YOLOv8n) and 4,991 (YOLOv10n) of them, the 300th score was tied with the next. Which of the tied pairs are kept follows §2.3's fixed tie order.
  - The FP32 passes kept 176.4 and 144.0 per image on average. 1,556 and 1,135 images reached 300, and 2 of those were tied in each pass.
- **The passes' own checks:**
  - **Bound outputs:** on every 100th image, they equalled `rknn_outputs_get`'s.
  - **Determinism:** 0 of the first 50 images' outputs differed from §8 step 3's dumps.
  - **YOLOv8n's two pipeline post-processings:** NeonPost and `yolov8_post` gave the same detections on all 5,000 images.

**The pipeline's settings (§2.4):** the INT8 outputs post-processed as the pipeline runs them, at conf 0.25. They are evaluated on COCO's still images and are not comparable with published values.

| Model | Post-processing | AP@[.5:.95] | AP50 | AR@100 | Detections per image |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YOLOv8n | NeonPost, conf 0.25, NMS 0.45 | 30.6 | 41.4 | 35.4 | 4.86 |
| YOLOv10n | `yolov10_post`, conf 0.25, no NMS | 30.2 | 39.9 | 34.6 | 4.23 |

| Model | `P_cap`, 3 cores, with its post-processing | Raw rate, 1 core | `rknn_run` at `P_cap` | Post-processing at `P_cap` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YOLOv8n | 193.17 /s (NeonPost) | 69.21 /s | 15.38 ms | 0.128 ms |
| YOLOv10n | 161.84 /s (`yolov10_post`) | 57.91 /s | 18.41 ms | 0.108 ms |

- **YOLOv10n's `P_cap` is 16% lower.** Its `rknn_run` takes about 3 ms longer, and its post-processing about 0.02 ms less.

  C10's runs at the auxiliary point were still made. Rule 3 of PROTOCOL §9.3 does not judge them, and the comparison there is between unequal loads.

## The pipeline

Both arms are variant C, from the same binary. In each table, C10 − C8 is the mean of the three paired differences C10_r − C8_r, with its descriptive 95% t interval (2 degrees of freedom). No threshold is set.

### 8 channels × 5 fps, staggered (both unsaturated)

| Metric | C8 | C10 | C10 − C8 [95% t interval] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Process CPU | 17.797 ± 0.570 s | 18.070 ± 1.031 s | +0.273 s [−3.698, +4.245], or +1.73% [−20.37%, +23.82%] |
| System CPU, `/proc/schedstat`, minus idle | 20.043 ± 0.480 s | 20.374 ± 1.058 s | +0.331 s [−3.490, +4.152], or +1.78% |
| Latency p50 | 16.93 ms | 19.79 ms | +2.87 ms [+2.82, +2.91] |
| Latency p99 | 17.66 ± 0.07 ms | 21.02 ± 0.87 ms | +3.36 ms [+1.25, +5.47] |
| `rknn_run` p50 | 15.02 ms | 17.92 ms | +2.89 ms [+2.81, +2.98] |
| Post-processing p50 | 0.119 ms | 0.091 ms | −0.029 ms [−0.032, −0.025] |

- **Process CPU:** the paired differences are +1.48, +0.88 and −1.54 s, so their interval spans 0 widely. Neither arm's CV is above 10% (C8 3.2%, C10 5.7%).
- **Latency:** almost all of C10's extra latency is its longer `rknn_run`. Every channel of every run inferred 5.00 fps, and no frame was dropped.
- **The sampler:** the arrival phases spread over 161.2 and 158.5 ms of each 200 ms. The stagger error averaged 5.7 and 8.8 ms.

### 5 channels × 30 fps (C10 saturated)

| Metric | C8 | C10 | C10 − C8 [95% t interval] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Process CPU | 33.250 ± 1.051 s | 35.037 ± 0.999 s | +1.787 s [−0.765, +4.339], or +5.41% [−2.59%, +13.41%] |
| System CPU, `/proc/schedstat`, minus idle | 36.144 ± 1.253 s | 38.221 ± 1.227 s | +2.077 s [−1.245, +5.399], or +5.80% |
| Latency p50 | 17.92 ms | 24.95 ms | +7.04 ms [+5.42, +8.65] |
| Latency p99 | 23.87 ± 3.79 ms | 29.95 ± 1.89 ms | +6.08 ms [+0.04, +12.12] |
| `rknn_run` p50 | 15.52 ms | 18.81 ms | +3.29 ms [+3.16, +3.42] |
| Wait for an NPU worker (Q2) p50 | 0.008 ms | 3.919 ms | +3.91 ms [+0.98, +6.85] |
| Post-processing p50 | 0.127 ms | 0.110 ms | −0.017 ms [−0.022, −0.011] |

- **Process CPU:** the paired differences are +0.68, +1.97 and +2.71 s, all positive, and the interval still spans 0. CVs are 3.2% and 2.9%.
- **Latency:** about 3.3 ms of C10's extra latency is `rknn_run`. Most of the rest is frames waiting for an NPU worker. C8's median frame does not wait (0.008 ms), though its p99 wait is 6.6 ms.
  - At the median `rknn_run`, 150 inferences/s keep the three NPU cores busy about 94% of the time for C10 and about 78% for C8 (150 × 18.81 ms / 3 against 150 × 15.52 ms / 3). This is arithmetic on the medians, not a measurement.
  - This is what the saturated label stands for.
- **Throughput held:** every channel of every run still inferred 30.00 fps, and no frame was dropped.

### Where C10's extra CPU may be (exploratory)

CPU by thread group, from `threads.csv`, with the groups of M2's report:

| Point | Group | C8 | C10 | C10 − C8 [95% t interval] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 × 5 | NPU workers (`npu-*`) | 5.840 ± 0.090 s | 5.993 ± 0.049 s | +0.153 s [+0.010, +0.297] |
| | Decoder (`dec-*`, `mpp_*`) | 11.377 ± 0.497 s | 11.543 ± 1.074 s | +0.167 s [−3.719, +4.053] |
| 5 × 30 | NPU workers (`npu-*`) | 23.247 ± 0.538 s | 24.830 ± 0.348 s | +1.583 s [+0.560, +2.606] |
| | Decoder (`dec-*`, `mpp_*`) | 8.583 ± 0.441 s | 8.767 ± 0.590 s | +0.183 s [−1.226, +1.592] |

- **The NPU workers' threads used more CPU with C10** at both points, by +0.153 s [+0.010, +0.297] and +1.583 s [+0.560, +2.606].
  - Divided by a window's inferences (4,800 and 18,000), the means come to about 0.032 and 0.088 ms per inference.
  - This is arithmetic on means of 3 pairs, not a measured cost per inference.
- **The post-processing stage is shorter with C10,** and the binding is unchanged within 0.005 ms.
  - The extra CPU is therefore consistent with work inside the `rknn_run` call, which is also about 3 ms longer. The runtime is known to do work on the CPU inside that call (A6).
  - Thread CPU does not show which call the time is spent in. This report does not measure what that work is, nor why it would grow with YOLOv10n.
- **The decoder's threads** do the same work in both arms and carry most of the run-to-run spread at 8 × 5. This is as in M2.

## Threats to validity

- **One board, 3 runs per arm and point.** With 2 degrees of freedom the intervals are wide. The CPU differences at both points have intervals that span 0.
- **One export and one quantization per model.** Both used the same 20 quantization images, which are among the 5,000 evaluated. The values without them hardly differ. Another export, calibration set or toolkit can give other results (§7).
- **The accuracy is that of still images** prepared on the PC with a fixed 640 × 640 letterbox. The pipeline's inputs are video frames prepared by RGA, which M4 does not evaluate.
- **The published values** come from other settings (rectangular inputs, other exports). They are context, not a check of the conversion.
- **C10 is saturated at 5 × 30.** That comparison is between unequal loads.
- **The rules were chosen after an exploration** on 21 images, disclosed in the plan. The settings fixed there, the letterbox, thresholds, caps, tie order and COCOeval parameters, were not changed afterwards.
- **`/proc/stat`'s system CPU is noisy** (sd up to 32 s per point), as M1.1 found. The `/proc/schedstat` values are reported beside it; both are in the tables.
- **Ambient was reported, not measured:** 24 °C for the `calib_npu` session, 25 °C for the pipeline sessions.

## What M4 does not tell (§7)

- **About other images:** only COCO val2017's still images, prepared on the PC.
- **About the models in general:** only these exports and this quantization.
- **Which model is better:** M4 has no decision rule and makes no claim of one.

git diff --exit-code docs/report                       # the same file
