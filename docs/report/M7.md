# M7 report: the four cells at the auxiliary condition, the decoder's threads, and the kernel's side

**What M7 is:** two sweeps of `bench_main`, each in a session of its own, both with a 120 s window: `m7_main` at H1's point (8 channels, 5 inferences per second each) and `m7_aux` at the auxiliary condition (5 channels, 30 inferences per second each). Every arm also took two snapshots of what else ran on the board. The arms, the two hypotheses, the metrics and the order were registered before any run ([prereg/M7.md](../../prereg/M7.md), [A18](../../prereg/AMENDMENTS.md)).

| Arm | Sweep | What ran |
|---|---|---|
| B, Bd, Rc, C | both | M6's four cells ([M6](M6.md)): B converts on the CPU and copies into the NPU's input; Bd writes B's conversion straight into the NPU's slots; Rc converts on RGA3 and the CPU copies; C is the zero-copy path |
| Ct | `m7_aux` | C with the thread CPU time of every `rknn_run` recorded (`--time-rknn on`) |
| Cpt | `m7_aux` | Ct with the package's post-processing (`--variant C --post neon --time-rknn on`) |
| Bk, Bdk, Rck, Ck | `m7_main` | the four cells with hardware counters on every pipeline thread: **not run**, because their check failed before `freeze-m7` (below) |

Every arm that ran had `--snapshot on`.

**Outcome: both hypotheses are adopted. The four counted arms were not run.**

| Hypothesis | Value | Threshold | Spec-literal | Practical | Same value from the other source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M7-H1:** at 5 channels × 30 fps, Rc keeps at least 90% of C's saving against B, `(B − Rc) / (B − C)` | 0.9618 | ≥ 0.90 | **adopted** | **adopted** | 0.9592 on `/proc/schedstat`: not *mixed* |
| **M7-H2:** at 8 channels, C's decoder threads use at least 0.50 s more CPU than Bd's, `D(C) − D(Bd)` | 1.7433 s | ≥ 0.50 s | **adopted** | **adopted** | 1.7562 s from the snapshots, scaled to 120 s: not *mixed* |

M7-H1 is from the means of 3 runs of process CPU per window in `m7_aux`: B 132.413 s, Rc 41.590 s, C 37.987 s. M7-H2 is from the means of 3 runs of the decoder group's CPU in `m7_main`: Bd 10.030 s, C 11.773 s. No flag is set: every named arm has 3 runs that count, every channel ran at 30.000 fps (`m7_aux`) and 5.000 fps (`m7_main`) in every run, no frame was dropped, and no CV is above §4's 10%. The CVs of process CPU were B 0.92%, Rc 4.50% and C 3.91% at the auxiliary point; the decoder group's of Bd and C at H1's point are not printed in the tables, and from its mean and sd they are 0.8% and 2.4% (sd ÷ mean, computed here). Both adoptions were disclosed in advance as the expected results (M7 §2).

**The counters' check failed, so question 2 has no counters.** In the check run of Ck before `freeze-m7`, the NPU workers' cycles divided by 2208 MHz were 0.6396 s against a run time of 0.6778 s: 5.6% apart, outside the registered 5% ([A18](../../prereg/AMENDMENTS.md)). By M7 §5, Bk, Bdk, Rck and Ck are then not run. M7-H2 stands, and the thread CPU and the snapshots describe the decoder's threads, but nothing in M7 says whether C's decoder threads ran more instructions or the same ones more slowly; `Xk − X` and §5's 2% rule are missing with them. Why the counters fell short is not established: every group that ran counted fewer cycles than its run time, the decoder's by 5.0%. The criterion was not changed and the check was not run again.

**What M7 shows, in one line each:**

- **At 30 fps too, Rc kept most of C's saving against B:** 90.8 s of 94.4 s. `Rc − C` was 3.6 s, 0.200 ms per inference, between the plan's two expectations.
- **C's decoder threads used more CPU than Bd's in a third session:** 1.74 s at H1's point, after 1.04 s (M1.1) and 1.14 s (M6). Why is not known (above).
- **The copy bundle and the interaction moved.** `Rc − C` at H1's point was 0.73 s here against 1.60 s in M6. `(B − Bd) − (Rc − C)` was +0.78 s at H1's point and −1.72 s at the auxiliary one. Neither is tested.
- **The arms that convert on RGA3 ran RGA3's interrupt threads and more `kworker` time** (not attributed to a driver): in C, 0.20 s more than in B per 121 s span at H1's point and 0.77 s more at the auxiliary one; the `mpp_worker` threads ran less. Hard-interrupt time is not measured as a value of its own.
- **`rknn_run` costs about the same CPU per call at 30 fps** as M6 found at H1's point (1.16 against 1.21 ms), which here is more than half of Ct's process CPU. The package's post-processing did not change it measurably.
- **The probe accepted all six output bindings,** at 0.45 ms (the native layout) to 5.93 ms (NHWC FLOAT32) of thread CPU per `rknn_run`, against 0.84 ms for the pipeline's binding. That is one context running alone, not the pipeline.

## Threats to validity

- **One board, one image, one model, two points, one day.** File-backed RTSP streams at 1080p.
- **One session per sweep, three runs per arm.** The intervals are descriptive, from 3 pairs. Three orders cannot balance the arms (M7 §4); every run's position is in the tables. At H1's point B's and Rc's values rose through the session (question 2; Rc's r3, the top of its rise, had the hand read inside its window), and at the auxiliary point B's, Rc's and C's highest runs were in r1.
- **Both hypotheses were the expected results,** disclosed so in M7 §2. Seen before `freeze-m7`, the 10 s runs of A18 gave `(B − Rc) / (B − C)` = 0.965 at the auxiliary point ((10.93 − 3.30) / (10.93 − 3.02), computed here); A18 lists no `D`. The thresholds, the arms, the metrics and the validity rules were fixed by `prereg-m7`, before the code of M7's options existed.
- **M7-H2 says that the difference repeated, not why.** Without the counters, question 2 has only the thread CPU and the snapshots' counts.
- **The counters' check failed for a reason not established** (A18). The four arms were not run, as M7 §5 registered for that case; nothing about the counters was tuned or retried.
- **The snapshots' span is the window and about one second before it,** not rescaled. A process that starts and ends between the two snapshots is in neither; its time is only in the system CPU and the remainder. Threads in one snapshot only are listed in [the tables](M7_tables.md#runs-whose-snapshots-or-counters-are-missing-4): in the first only, `systemd-timedat` in every run and some `kworker` threads, whose time after the first moment is not known; in the second only, threads that ran at most 0.013 s.
- **Kernel threads are grouped by name at the second snapshot** (A18); a `kworker`'s time is not attributed to a driver.
- **Interrupts and soft interrupts are counts.** cpu0's counter covers one CPU, and its difference from `/proc/schedstat` holds idle entry and exit as well as interrupts.
- **Handling during the sessions** (see the disclosures): the hand reads of the log, the `du` during a warm-up, the process checks, which reached only some of `m7_aux`'s runs, and the idle baseline of `m7_aux`. They are outside M7 §4's validity rules; the affected runs' values are shown next to their repetitions'. One run's remainder (disclosure 7) is not explained.
- **The room temperature** is the operator's report, once for both sessions ([#68](https://github.com/eunkyo3/rk3588s-zero-copy/issues/68)).
- **A18's readings** decided none of the verdicts: no value is at a threshold's exact boundary, and both mixed labels are on the same side as their values by wide margins.

## What M7 does not tell (§7)

- **Time in hard interrupt handlers, as a value of its own:** `/proc/stat`'s `irq` column read 0, and M7 installs no tracing tool. Inside a task's time it is not separated; in idle time it is bounded loosely, on cpu0 only, by one counter.
- **Why the decoder's threads differ** between arms that decode the same streams. The counters that would have described it were not run, and even they would not have said which code.
- **A pure cost of "the copy":** each cell is a bundle (M6 §1). Nor why `Rc − C` at H1's point was smaller than in M6, or why `(B − Bd) − (Rc − C)` changes sign between the points.
- **What `rknn_run` does with its CPU time in the pipeline,** or whether any of the probe's bindings would save CPU there.
- **Counters at the auxiliary condition:** none were planned there, and none ran at H1's point either.
- **Why C's process CPU at H1's point was higher than in earlier sessions.**
- **Power and memory traffic** ([#58](https://github.com/eunkyo3/rk3588s-zero-copy/issues/58)), **other points, models, boards or software.**
- **Anything new about M1 to M6's verdicts:** they are not judged again. M7 answers [#62](https://github.com/eunkyo3/rk3588s-zero-copy/issues/62), [#59](https://github.com/eunkyo3/rk3588s-zero-copy/issues/59) and [#60](https://github.com/eunkyo3/rk3588s-zero-copy/issues/60) in part.
