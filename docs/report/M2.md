# M2 report: a faster post-processing (C against Cp)

**What M2 is:** one change to C, M1's zero-copy pipeline: a faster post-processing (arm **Cp**), with detections identical to M1's. It was pre-registered in [prereg/M2.md](../../prereg/M2.md) with two CPU hypotheses, and both arms ran in the same sessions from the same binary.

| Hypothesis | C | Cp | (C − Cp) / C | Threshold | Spec-literal | Practical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M2-H1:** at 8 channels, main condition, Cp's process CPU is at least 3% lower | 18.737 s | 17.600 s | **6.07%** | 3% | adopted | adopted |
| **M2-H2:** at 5 channels × 30 fps, at least 5% lower | 37.280 s | 31.790 s | **14.73%** | 5% | adopted | adopted |

Process CPU is the mean of 3 runs per 120 s window. No verdict is labelled *mixed*: the secondary metric, `/proc/schedstat`'s system CPU minus idle, moved the same way at both points, by 6.19% and 13.33%. M2-H1's paired differences vary more, and their descriptive interval spans 0 (below).

## What was compared

- **C:** M1's C with M1's post-processing, `core/yolov8_post.cpp`, unchanged.
- **Cp:** C with version 4 of the prototype of [m2_exploration.md](../notes/m2_exploration.md), in `core/yolov8_post_neon.cpp`.
  - The score sums and class scores are scanned with NEON, 16 cells at a time.
  - The box decoding's softmax terms come from a table of all 256 × 256 pairs of quantized bins, computed with M1's own expression.
  - The outputs are prefetched.
- **Everything else is the same:** the decoder, RGA preprocessing, slots, queues, threads, NPU bindings and `rknn_init` flags. Only the post-processing function differs.
- **Detections:** before the runs, Cp's detections equalled C's field for field. That covered every frame of videos ① to ④ (3,600 frames) and 1,000,000 synthetic box decodings, with the same compiled objects as the runs (A13).

## Results

### M2-H1: 8 channels, main condition

- **Process CPU:** C 18.737 ± 0.170 s, Cp 17.600 ± 0.560 s, so (C − Cp) / C = 6.07% against the threshold of 3%. Both verdicts are *adopted*.
- **Guards:** no frame was dropped, and every channel of every run inferred 5.00 fps.
- **Secondary metric:** `/proc/schedstat` gave 20.639 ± 0.238 s against 19.361 ± 0.622 s, so (C − Cp) / C = 6.19%, the same direction.
- **Per repetition:** (C_r − Cp_r) / C_r = 8.76%, 6.23% and 3.21%.
  - Their mean's descriptive 95% t-interval is −0.83% to 12.97%.
  - Cp's coefficient of variation, 3.18%, is above half the threshold (1.5%). So Cp is flagged (M2 §2).
  - The verdict compares the means with the threshold, as registered. The interval is reported, not tested.

### M2-H2: 5 channels × 30 fps

- **Process CPU:** C 37.280 ± 0.528 s, Cp 31.790 ± 0.401 s, so (C − Cp) / C = 14.73% against the threshold of 5%. Both verdicts are *adopted*.
- **Guards:** no frame was dropped, and every channel inferred 30.00 fps.
- **Secondary metric:** `/proc/schedstat` gave 40.389 ± 0.393 s against 35.004 ± 0.540 s, so (C − Cp) / C = 13.33%.
- **Per repetition:** 13.57%, 15.36% and 15.23%. The t-interval is 12.24% to 17.20%, and no arm is flagged.

Every value, per run too, is in [M2_tables.md](M2_tables.md).

## Where the saving comes from (exploratory)

CPU per thread group, mean of 3 runs, from `threads.csv`:

| Point | Arm | NPU workers (`npu-*`) | Decoder (`dec-*`, MPP) | Preprocessing (`pre-*`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 ch, main | C | 6.83 ± 0.08 s | 11.33 ± 0.15 s | 0.45 s |
| | Cp | 5.82 ± 0.01 s | 11.24 ± 0.53 s | 0.43 s |
| 5 ch, 30 fps | C | 27.10 ± 0.46 s | 8.74 ± 0.25 s | 1.31 s |
| | Cp | 22.15 ± 0.18 s | 8.29 ± 0.29 s | 1.21 s |

- **Most of the CPU difference is in the NPU workers' threads,** where post-processing runs. Its stage took 0.12 ms at the median against 0.35 to 0.36 ms.
- **Per inference:** Cp saved 0.237 ms at the main point (4,800 inferences) and 0.305 ms at the auxiliary point (18,000). The prototype had saved 0.232 ms per call.
  - At the auxiliary point the NPU workers alone saved 0.275 ms per inference, more than the post-processing stage's own drop.
  - This report does not explain the difference.
- **Cp's flag at the main point is carried by the decoder's threads,** which do the same work in both arms. Their sd was 0.53 s for Cp, nearly all of Cp's 0.56 s, and their mean did not differ between the arms.
- **Against the projection** made before the thresholds: 5.7% projected, 6.07% measured at the main point; 11.1% projected, 14.73% measured at the auxiliary point.

## The detections during the runs (§4, no verdict)

- **The registered comparison** matched each frame by its `pts` modulo the length of the channel's video. It found different detection counts on 314 of 653, 0 of 1,839 and 306 of 1,029 frames (main, r1 to r3), and on 1,981 to 2,715 of 4,786 (auxiliary).
- **Investigated:** the cause is the matching, not the detections.
  - A run's `pts` start where its reader joined the stream, not at the video's first frame. So `pts` names the same frame only within a run.
  - For each channel and repetition, exactly one shift of Cp's frame numbers, between −15 and +15 frames, makes every compared frame's count agree (39 of 39 channel-repetitions). Any other shift agrees on at most 53%.
  - This is an after-the-fact reading. The check before the runs (§4) stands as the evidence that Cp's detections equal C's.

## Threats to validity

- **One board, 3 runs per arm and point.**
  - At the main point the effect is small next to the run-to-run variation of the decoder's threads, and the paired differences' interval spans 0.
  - At the auxiliary point the effect is much larger than that variation.
- **The thresholds were set after the prototype** had been timed. That is disclosed, and they are about half of its projection. Both measured savings are above the projection.
- **The staggered sampler changes the main condition for both arms.** M2's main-condition numbers are not M1's condition.
- **Process CPU at 8 channels** includes about 11 s of decoder work that Cp does not change. The relative saving depends on that share.
- **The board reset itself once** during a build before the checks (A13). The cause is unknown. Every run afterwards was valid at fixed clocks.
- **Ambient was reported, not measured:** 28 °C.
